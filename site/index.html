The New Year's plan

Day by day
Wed · Dec 30, 2026

Arrival + Christmas lights

Evening walk through the Alumbrados, Medellín's famous Christmas lights along the river at Parques del Río. About 1.5 km of pedestrian displays, on from 6 pm to midnight. Easy and magical for the kids.

  • Where Parques del Río
  • Hours 6 pm to midnight
  • 2026 to 2027 dates TBC

TBC: the 2026 to 2027 dates are confirmed by EPM closer to December. Last season the lights ran to Jan 12.
Ops: price a private driver for the evening (hotel to Parques del Río, then return).

Thu · Dec 31, 2026

New Year's Eve

Morning

Pueblito Paisa on Cerro Nutibara, a replica of a traditional Antioquian village with views over the city. Dec 31 hours unconfirmed

Afternoon

Pool and rest. Pick up a "New Year's kit" for local traditions: yellow underwear for good luck, 12 grapes, and a small suitcase.

Evening

Early family New Year's Eve dinner at the Marriott Medellín or the InterContinental Medellín. Pricing & timing TBC
Ops: check both, get prices for adults and for kids ages 3 and 5, and the timing. 2026 programs are usually published in November.

Midnight

Fireworks from the hotel rooftop as the whole valley lights up. Eat the 12 grapes, one per chime, and walk around the block with the suitcase to wish for a year of travel.

Before the night
  • Fireworks are loud from the afternoon until late. Bring ear protection for the kids.
  • Use only a private driver or app-based transport that night.
  • Book everything in advance. Many restaurants close Dec 31 and Jan 1.
Fri · Jan 1, 2027

Slow day

Late breakfast, pool, and an easy walk around El Poblado. Most places are closed on New Year's Day.

  • Try: Pergamino Coffee

Don't leave without a coffee at Pergamino, or a bag to take home. They have several locations.

Sat · Jan 2, 2027

Guatapé and El Peñol

El Peñol rock and the colorful town of Guatapé. Jan 2 is a Saturday, so start early to beat the crowds. We recommend the boat tour over the 740 steps to the top.
